What we supply

The whole frame, built before it ships.

Everything above the foundation, framed in cold-formed steel. Our scope is design, engineering, framing, sheathing and decking. Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, insulation and finishes stay with your trades.

How it works

From drawings to a standing frame.

Every stud is modeled before it’s made. Panels are built in the order they’ll be assembled, so the site crew stands walls instead of measuring and cutting them.

  1. Design

    Your drawings or ours, converted into a full digital model of the frame.

  2. Engineer

    Engineered to your codes and your wind, snow and seismic loads.

  3. Fabricate

    Roll-formed, framed, sheathed and decked in a controlled factory.

  4. Kit & deliver

    Numbered, labeled and shipped in install sequence.

  5. Stand

    Our crew or yours. Panels stand in days, not weeks.

  6. Hand-off

    Your trades take the dried-in frame from there.
