Robert Johnson

Vice President of Strategy and Prefabrication · Statement of Qualifications

Robert Johnson is an eminent figure in the commercial and residential construction and landscapes, boasting over 40 years of extensive experience, with a significant focus on prefabricated cold-formed steel/metal stud framing. His career is marked by pivotal leadership roles, including spearheading the initiation of a prefabricated cold-formed steel manufacturing operation and overseeing the management of an existing establishment in this sector. These roles have not only demonstrated his exceptional operational leadership and project management skills but have also deeply immersed him in the intricacies of prefabricated construction methodologies.

Robert's tenure as a primary contact, estimator, and business development lead for numerous residential, mid-rise multi-family and hospitality projects across North America has further cemented his reputation. This experience has not only honed his skills in estimating and business development but also underscored his proficiency in cultivating and nurturing essential industry relationships, thereby enhancing his comprehensive understanding of the prefabricated cold-formed steel construction sector.

Currently aligning with several roll former manufacturers, each being frontrunners in providing software and roll-forming equipment for the prefabricated cold-formed steel sectors, Robert has continued to leverage his vast expertise. He continues to be instrumental in pushing the boundaries of construction technology and practices, focusing on the prefabrication domain and ensuring his engagement with the sector's latest advancements.

Moreover, as the host of the “Build it Better Podcast,” Robert has conversed with hundreds of industry professionals, ranging from engineers and designers to developers, architects, and suppliers. This unique platform has enabled him to share his profound insights and knowledge, fostering a culture of learning and collaboration within the construction community.

A pivotal aspect of Robert Johnson's career is his profound understanding and knowledge of prefabricated cold-formed steel standard practices, means, and methods. This expertise is particularly relevant and invaluable in contexts requiring mediation and negotiation, where his opinions are grounded in decades of hands-on experience and leadership within the construction and prefabrication sectors. Robert's deep familiarity with the standard practices, means, and methods of prefabricated cold-formed steel construction positions him as an authoritative figure capable of offering expert opinions.

Robert Johnson's career not only illustrates his dedication and leadership in the construction industry but also underscores his capacity to provide expert opinions and guidance on prefabricated cold-formed steel standard practices. His blend of operational experience, industry engagement, and a forward-looking approach to technology and construction processes renders him uniquely qualified to contribute authoritative and informed perspectives, further enhancing the development and application of best practices in the sector.
